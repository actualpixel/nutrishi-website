FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else? Write to support@nutrishi.com.

Is Nutrishi free?

Yes. Food logging, the Indian food database, barcode scanning, and water and weight tracking are all free to use.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start logging. There’s no sign-up, email or password.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. Your food logs, weight and profile stay on your device unless you export a backup or share it yourself.

How accurate are the calories?

They’re estimates. Home-cooked Indian food varies with oil, recipe and portion size, so Nutrishi gives practical estimates rather than false precision. For packaged food, the label on the pack is the best reference.

What if my food isn’t listed?

Add it in seconds with manual entry and save it for next time. You can also email support@nutrishi.com and ask us to add it.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Logging, the food database and your history all work without internet. Looking up a new barcode needs a connection.
