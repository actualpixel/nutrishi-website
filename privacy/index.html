Data stored on your phone

The following is stored on your device. The app does not send it to us or to anyone else:

  • Profile details: name, gender, age, height, weight, activity level, goal and diet preference
  • Calorie, macro, water and weight targets
  • Food logs, saved foods, custom foods, meal templates and products you have scanned
  • Meal plans, water logs and weight history
  • Reminder, appearance, App Lock and other app settings

The app uses this data on your device to estimate your targets, track your day, show progress and schedule reminders you turn on.

This data is not encrypted by Nutrishi itself. App Lock protects access to the app, not the stored files. On iPhone, app data can be included in your own iCloud or computer backups if you use them, and exported backup files and automatic pre-import safety copies are visible in the Files app.

If you uninstall the app, clear its data or use Reset local data, this information is deleted from your phone. Export a backup first if you want to keep it.

Usage data (optional)

With your permission, the app sends us events about how features are used, such as “a meal was logged” or “a barcode scan found a product”. This helps us see which features help people keep tracking and where they get stuck.

It is off until you allow it. The app asks you once. You can change your choice at any time in Profile → Data & Privacy → Share usage data.

What is sent

  • The name of the action, for example “food logged”, “water logged” or “meal template created”
  • Limited details about how it was done, for example where in the app it started, the meal slot, whether a template was used, the food category chosen (such as “Breakfast”), a water amount range (such as “250–499 ml”), or whether a search or barcode lookup found a result
  • Your platform (Android or iOS) on some events
  • The time of the event and a random identifier created for your app install. The identifier lets us count events from the same install over time. It is not your name, phone number or advertising ID, and we do not link it to you.

What is never sent

  • Food or meal names, calories, macros, weight or other body measurements
  • Your name, age, gender or diet preference
  • Search text, barcode numbers or anything you type
  • Your contacts, GPS location or advertising ID

Like any internet connection, your device’s IP address reaches PostHog when data is sent. The app instructs PostHog not to use it to estimate your location, and not to create a user profile from these events.

Turning it off stops collection straight away and deletes any usage events still waiting on your phone. An event that is already being sent at that moment may still arrive. If you turn it on again later, a new random install ID is used.

Retention and deletion. Usage events are kept for up to 12 months under our PostHog plan. After that, PostHog deletes them in its regular clean-up, which can take some extra time. Because these events are linked only to a random install ID and no user profile is created, PostHog’s tools do not currently let us find and delete one installation’s events on request, so they are removed when the retention period ends. You can contact our Grievance Officer with any question or request about this data.

Barcode lookups

When you scan a packaged product that isn’t already saved on your phone, the app sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free public database, to fetch the product’s name, nutrition details and product image. Like any internet request, this includes your IP address. No other app data is sent.

The camera is used to read the barcode on your phone; the app does not upload camera images. Product details are saved on your phone so the same product works offline next time. Open Food Facts handles requests under its own privacy policy.

The barcode scanner uses Google’s ML Kit, which reads barcodes on your phone. According to Google’s documentation, ML Kit may send Google technical information: your device model, operating system and app version, scanner settings, performance and error data, and an identifier for this installation. Google’s list of this data does not include camera images or barcode values, and the app gives ML Kit no other Nutrishi data. Google’s library decides when this is sent; it cannot be switched off in the app and is separate from the optional usage data above. We cannot access or delete this data; Google handles it under its own terms.

Who we share data with

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. Data reaches these parties only as described here:

  • PostHog (analytics service provider): usage events, only if you allow them
  • Open Food Facts (public product database): the barcode number of a product you look up
  • Google (ML Kit): technical scanner data, as described under Barcode lookups
  • Our email provider and GitHub (service providers): to run support and host nutrishi.com
  • Government or law enforcement authorities: only when Indian law requires it

PostHog stores data in the USA. Indian law allows this unless the Government restricts transfers to a country; we will follow any such restriction.

Security and incidents

  • Your health and body data stays on your device rather than on our servers.
  • App Lock can protect access to the app with your phone’s face, fingerprint or passcode. Nutrishi does not store your biometric data; your phone’s operating system handles it.
  • The app connects to PostHog and Open Food Facts over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Usage events are limited to an approved list and checked in the app before they are sent. Access to them is limited to the Nutrishi team.

Exported backup files contain your personal nutrition and health information in readable form. Keep them private and store them securely.

If a personal data breach affects data we handle, we will notify affected users and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law.

Your rights and how to use them

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Get a summary of the personal data we process about you, and who we share it with
  • Correct, complete or update your data
  • Have your data erased
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Have your complaints addressed
  • Nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or are unable to

How to use them

  • Data on your phone: view and edit it in the app, export it from Profile → Data & Privacy → Export my data, or erase it with Reset local data. We cannot access this data, so you control it directly.
  • Usage data: turn off Share usage data in Profile → Data & Privacy. This stops collection and deletes events not yet sent from your phone. Events already received by PostHog cannot be deleted individually; see “Retention and deletion” above.
  • Emails you sent us: ask our Grievance Officer to delete them, and we will, unless the law requires us to keep them.
  • Anything else, including nomination: email our Grievance Officer at support@actualpixel.in.

We will respond as soon as possible, and in any case within 90 days.

Children

Nutrishi is not intended for children under 13, and the app does not accept a profile age below 13. If the age in your profile is under 18, usage-data sharing stays off and cannot be turned on.

If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern about your child’s data, contact our Grievance Officer.

Phone permissions

  • Camera: only to scan barcodes, when you open the scanner.
  • Notifications: only for reminders you turn on. Reminders are scheduled on your phone.
  • Face ID, fingerprint or passcode: only if you turn on App Lock.

You can withdraw any of these in your phone’s settings at any time.
